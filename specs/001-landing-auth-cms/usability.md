## Protocol for SC-015 (five people per language)

1. Recruit five first-time visitors who read English and five who read Arabic,
   ideally on their own phones. Nobody who worked on the site.
2. Open the production or staging site in their language. Do not explain it.
3. Task: "You need someone to photograph a birthday in Sharjah next Friday for
   two hours. Ask for it." Stop when WhatsApp opens or after three minutes.
4. Record: completed without help (yes/no), attempts at the primary action,
   taps, time, and one sentence on where they hesitated.
5. Pass: at least four of five per language complete without help, and nobody
   needs more than one attempt at the Top Hit.
